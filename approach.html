Finance built with ownership

We don't believe in simply completing a checklist of finance activities. We believe someone should take ownership of the finance function — understanding the numbers, the business, and what management needs to know.

How We Work

Our ownership-driven approach

01 · Understand

We start with the business — not the accounting software. We understand your business model, revenue drivers, cost structure, people, customers, systems and growth plans.

02 · Diagnose

We identify gaps across accounting, reporting, cash flow, controls, processes, technology, KPIs and planning — then prioritise what needs to be fixed first.

03 · Build

We build the finance foundation for your stage of growth — reporting packs, financial models, cash forecasts, KPI dashboards, SOPs, controls and automation.

04 · Operate

We establish a finance rhythm — monthly close, management reporting, cash reviews, budget vs actuals, forecast updates, KPI reviews and management meetings.

05 · Improve

As the business changes, finance changes with it. We continuously improve profitability, cash conversion, processes, controls, automation, reporting and decision-making.
